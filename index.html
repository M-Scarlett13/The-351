How to play

  • 01Letters
    A letter is drawn. You get 45 seconds to name every city or town that starts with it. 10 points each.
  • 02Name that town
    One town glows on the map. Pick it from eight nearby options in three guesses or fewer.
  • 03Time Machine
    A moment from Massachusetts history. Guess the year. Closer counts for more.
  • 04Distance
    How far apart two towns sit, or how long a town's border runs. Scored on how close you land.
Everyone gets the same four rounds each day. A new puzzle arrives at midnight. Your progress saves as you go, so you can put it down and come back.
